Computer Engineer — AI/ML Developer & Researcher, Qatar University

MK

Mohammad Kasif is a computer engineering graduate from Qatar University, based in Doha, Qatar. His work sits at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, LLM systems, materials informatics, and research engineering. He has worked across QCRI, QEERI, QSTP, HMC, and Qatar University research projects, building practical AI systems and research pipelines.

My work has several connected threads: LLM and NL2SQL evaluation, computer vision for medical and classroom settings, materials informatics for organic thermoelectric polymers, and deployment-oriented AI systems. I enjoy building systems that move from research ideas into working prototypes, experiments, demos, and papers.

Vision. I believe strong AI engineers should understand both theory and implementation. My goal is to build systems that are not only accurate, but explainable, useful, and grounded in real-world constraints. I am especially interested in deep theoretical computer science, machine learning foundations, and research problems where software engineering meets scientific discovery.

research directions

  • LLMs, NL2SQL, and evaluation systems
  • Computer vision and deep learning for real-world settings
  • Materials informatics and physics-informed machine learning
  • Research engineering, MLOps, and deployable AI systems
